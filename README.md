Whiskers in the Shadows

A PSX-style first-person horror game built in Unity 6 for my final year project.
You play as a cat searching for your missing owner. What starts as a quiet exploration through a neighbourhood turns into something much darker.

About

Whiskers in the Shadows is a short horror experience inspired by PSX-era games like Silent Hill and the work of Puppet Combo. The game uses a custom URP rendering pipeline (URP-PSX-FORKED) to achieve pixelation, CRT scanlines, dithering, vertex snapping and custom fog — all the visual hallmarks of late 90s PlayStation horror.
The game was built solo over the course of a final year project, developed iteratively in four stages: Prototype, Alpha, Beta and Final.

Gameplay

Play as a cat exploring a neighbourhood, forest and mansion
Follow scent trails using E to interact with clues and Q to re-show the active trail
Survive two BagMan encounters with cinematic reveal sequences and chase mechanics
Reach the bedroom discovery and make your way home
Experience the sleep ending

Controls: WASD to move, Shift to run, Space to jump, E to interact, Q to re-show scent trail, ESC to pause

Technical Features

PSX rendering pipeline via URP-PSX-FORKED (pixelation, dithering, CRT, fog, vertex snapping)
Custom PSX camera follow system with sphere cast collision avoidance and front mode for chase sequences
Director-based cinematic system covering BagMan reveals, road cutscene, bedroom discovery, home return and sleep ending
Scent trail system with particle spawning, timed visibility and Q re-show
BagMan AI with waypoint patrol, rush-to-point chase and house patrol
Checkpoint and respawn system
Eyelid open/close system used across intro, wake sequence and sleep ending
Glitch effect system for the final ending sequence
Full main menu and pause menu with keyboard navigation and volume control
Tutorial prompt with pixel scramble reveal effect
Atmospheric audio system with zone-based footsteps, wind fades and spatial 3D audio
